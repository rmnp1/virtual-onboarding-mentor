# Work Policies

## Working Hours
- Flexible working hours with core hours 10:00–15:00
- 8 hours per day (40 hours per week)
- Remote work: 2 days per week (after probation period)

## Leave
- **Vacation** — 26 days per calendar year
- **On-demand leave** — 4 days per year (included in vacation pool)
- **Sick leave** — standard medical leave policies apply
- **Training leave** — possibility to take leave for courses or certifications

## Benefits
- Private healthcare (Medicover)
- Multisport card
- Life insurance
- Training budget (5000 PLN/year)
- Company equipment (laptop + monitor)
- Fruit Tuesdays and beer Fridays

## Promotion Process
1. Self-assessment (every 6 months)
2. Manager review
3. Setting goals for the next period
4. Promotion or raise based on performance

## Code of Conduct
- Respect for colleagues and coworkers
- Transparent communication — don't hide problems
- Responsibility for your own tasks
- Helping other team members
