# Company Overview

## Our Mission
We are a technology company delivering innovative solutions for businesses. Our goal is digitizing processes and increasing operational efficiency for our clients.

## Organizational Structure
The company is divided into the following departments:
- **Engineering** — software development and infrastructure
- **Product** — product management and roadmap
- **Marketing** — promotion and communication
- **Sales** — sales and client relations
- **HR** — human resources and recruitment
- **Finance** — finances and accounting
- **Operations** — internal operations and logistics

## Company Values
1. Innovation — we always look for better solutions
2. Collaboration — we work together toward common goals
3. Transparency — open communication at every level
4. Growth — we invest in our employees' development

## Office Location
Our main office is located in the city center. We also have remote offices in several locations. Hybrid work is standard here — 3 days in office, 2 days remote.
