# Onboarding Procedures

## First Day
1. Meeting with your buddy — overview of the day's plan
2. Equipment pickup (laptop, monitor, keyboard)
3. Setting up company account and system access
4. Introduction to core tools: Slack, Jira, Git
5. Team standup meeting

## First Week
- Review technical documentation
- System architecture overview
- First small task (starter assignment)
- 1:1 meeting with your manager
- Benefits and work policies overview

## First Month
- Independent task execution
- Code review with the team
- Summary meeting with your manager
- 360° feedback from the team

## Important Contacts
- **Buddy** — assigned by your manager at the first meeting
- **HR** — questions about benefits, leave, formalities
- **IT Helpdesk** — hardware and access issues
- **Manager** — team and development matters
